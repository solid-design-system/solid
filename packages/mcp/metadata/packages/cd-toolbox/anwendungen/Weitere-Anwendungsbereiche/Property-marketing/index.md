# Property marketing

The aim of property marketing is to successfully position a property on the market. In many cases, a unique and individual presentation is vital to highlight and reinforce its advantages, its own identity and distinctiveness in relation to competing properties. Successful property marketing not only strengthens the image of the property, but also that of the Union Investment brand as the owner or the property. It is important to understand that the property is its own brand with its own market and specific, independent needs. Its success must be the top priority in property marketing. The Union Investment brand is an important quality feature of the property.
